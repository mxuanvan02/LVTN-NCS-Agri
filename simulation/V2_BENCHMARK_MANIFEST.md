## v2 primary design (verified from supplied artifacts)

- 2 plants: `greenhouse`, `irrigation`
- 4 policies: `TT-MPC`, `ET-MPC`, `TT-PI`, `ET-PI`
- 6 networks: `N0_ideal` through `N5_full_stress`
- 50 seeds: 2026--2075
- 2,400 scheduled/completed runs; 0 failed (`results/v2_provenance.json`)
- Forecast: causal persistence; oracle is forbidden from primary analysis
- Primary outcomes: NRMSE, violation percentage, transmissions, total modeled energy (mJ), command-applied percentage, deadline-miss percentage
- Sensitivity: 1,800 separate ET-MPC runs; exploratory and not primary inference. A reproducible descriptive summary is generated by `experiments/report_v2_sensitivity.py`; oracle occurs only in this sensitivity branch.
- Multiplicity: raw paired p-values and unadjusted 95% t-CIs are retained. Holm is applied within each `network × metric` endpoint to the four preregistered plant-controller-family contrasts (2 plants × MPC/PI). Holm adjusts p-values only; the CIs and decision gates are not simultaneous family-wise intervals.
- Decision gates: regenerated for both preregistered controller-family contrasts, giving 24 rows (2 plants × 2 controller families × 6 networks). All 12 greenhouse rows pass and all 12 irrigation rows fail solely because the lower CI for transmission reduction is below 20%; the NRMSE and violation guardrails do not bind in this dataset.
- Endpoint caveats: `deadline_miss_pct` is identically zero and therefore non-discriminating under the configured deadlines; `command_applied_pct` uses each policy's attempted transmissions as denominator and is not used in the gate.
- Random-tape scope: the stored digest covers process/sensor-noise arrays and the seed/plant/network key. Deterministic keyed channel draws are reproducible but are not individually enumerated in that digest.
- Integrity manifests have distinct scopes: `results/v2_output_hashes.json` verifies 11/11 frozen v2 outputs; `SHA256SUMS.final.txt` covers the publishable whole bundle and intentionally excludes itself, the historical initial manifest, virtual environments, caches, bytecode, and Git metadata.
- Scope: software simulation and SIL/HIL-ready interfaces; no field or hardware validation
